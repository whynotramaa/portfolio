## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a 35mm contact sheet marked up in red grease pencil. It refuses the cream-serif editorial page and the dark neon dev portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Cool photographic paper ground, film-black strips with sprocket holes and tiny edge-print data in mono, one red grease pencil (Peehu) that circles, ticks and scrawls. Archivo variable (width + weight axes) for display and body. Light theme is "lights on", dark theme is the darkroom under safelight red.

STORY: The visitor meets ramaa as five exposed frames, laughs at the pasta line, skims seven projects as numbered frames that develop on hover, wanders the light table, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed film strip holds r-a-m-a-a, one letter per frame, frame numbers 1A to 5A on the rebate; a red Peehu circle rings the last frame with "or formally, ramnath". Below on paper: the pasta joke large with "best jokes" circled in red, blurb, meta, say hello as the primary button, top left of the fold.

FORM: Darkroom contact sheet, position 4 of the grounded list, seed key 98dcc81a (re-roll 1). Signature interaction: a loupe cursor that magnifies labels and a hover "develop" that turns a project row into a black negative strip. Motion grammar: frames expose on load, sections develop from blur, drops slap down.
