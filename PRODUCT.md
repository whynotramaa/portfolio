# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering managers hiring a new-grad software engineer for 2027. They skim for about 90 seconds, want to know who this person is, and click into one or two projects before deciding to reach out.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Ramnath ("ramaa"), final-year B.Tech CSE at NIT Rourkela, graduating 2027, looking for a job. Success is a hiring person remembering the personality and emailing hire.ramaa@gmail.com or opening GitHub.

## Positioning

A builder with product taste and a sense of humour. The personality (jokes, handwriting, off-the-clock world) is the differentiator, backed by seven real shipped projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16, React 19, plain CSS in `app/globals.css`. No other runtime dependencies.
- Home page sections: hero, ticker, facts, about, selected work, stack, scrapbook world, distance, guestbook, contact, footer. Blog route at `/blog` (currently empty).
- Features that must survive: draggable scrapbook desk, IP-based distance card (ipapi.co), guestbook signature canvas stored in localStorage, nerd mode (element outlines + dev comments), dark/light theme with no-flash script.

## Brand Commitments

- Lowercase voice, playful, self-deprecating. Copy stays as written, especially the jokes and intro ("I make worst pasta and best jokes.").
- Peehu handwritten font (`public/fonts/Peehu-Regular.woff2`) is kept for handwritten asides.
- "made with zero em dashes" footer line.

## Evidence on Hand

- Projects: `lib/projects.ts` (Howzat, Shardly, PaperCode, Kairo, PlayWhatever, MovieFlix & Chill, FCSG-Net), with live links.
- Stack: `lib/stack.ts`. World cutouts: `lib/world.ts`.
- No project screenshots, no photos of the person, no testimonials. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Personality first, proof right behind it.
2. Skimmable in 90 seconds by someone hiring.
3. Every playful detail should reward a second look without blocking the first.
